Foreign Workforce & Visa Services

We recruit foreign specialists for Mongolian employers and manage their work-permit and visa process from application to arrival.

How it works

One team, both sides of the process

Whether you're a company that needs a specialist you can't hire locally, or a foreign professional looking for work in Mongolia, we manage sourcing, paperwork and compliance so nothing falls between the cracks.

The process

From request to arrival

Needs assessment

We define the role, requirements and quota position with you before sourcing begins.

Sourcing & screening

Candidates are sourced through our international network and screened against your criteria.

Interviews & selection

We coordinate interviews and support you through final candidate selection.

Work permit application

We prepare and submit the work-permit application with the relevant labour authority on your behalf.

Visa application

Once the permit is approved, we coordinate the work-visa application with the candidate.

Arrival & registration

We support arrival logistics, residence registration and onboarding into the role.
